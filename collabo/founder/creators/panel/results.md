# Buyer panel: Collabo (creator side): a marketplace where creators sell brand-deal packages, get paid through escrow, and get growth tools

**7 buy · 13 pass** (35% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 7, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- Proof that there are real brands on there booking creators my size at $1,000+ per post, so it brings me deals I wouldn't get on my own instead of taxing the ones I already have.
- Seeing real, named brands actively booking creators my size at decent rates, enough that my manager would want to use it too.
- Letting me run my existing brand deals through the escrow for a smaller cut, or proof that brands on there are booking 100K+ creators at $2K+ per post.
- No cut on deals I bring in myself (only charge on brands Collabo actually finds me), or proof it has enough paying brands to send me real bookings I wouldn't get otherwise.
- Seeing real brands on there posting briefs with budgets that match my tier, $2K or more per post, so it brings me better deals than my inbox does and isn't just a payment layer.
- Seeing real brands actually posting paid briefs for creators my size, like a friend under 5K getting booked and paid through it.
- Seeing real paid briefs from brands that want creators my size, or hearing from another small creator that she actually got booked and paid on Collabo.
- If real, bigger brands were posting briefs there at rates near what my manager gets me, and my manager could run the account so the escrow becomes a perk instead of an extra platform.
- Proof that brands are actually booking creators my size on it, like a feed of real briefs in my niche, or the hook analytics clearly beating what I already use so I'd come for growth and stay for deals.
- A real paid booking or a brand DMing me through it in the first couple weeks, like proof that small businesses on there actually pay creators my size.
- Seeing a real list of brands actively posting paid briefs for creators my size, or a friend under 5K actually getting paid through it.
- If my manager said deals I find on Collabo myself don't count toward their cut, or if it actually showed me brands booking creators my size every week.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
