Collabo: a website and phone app where you list brand-deal packages at your own prices (for example "1 TikTok, $500"), and small businesses book you or you pitch on their posted briefs.
The brand puts the full payment in escrow before you start. A draft approval releases 30% to you, the rest is released when the live post is approved, and if the brand doesn't respond within 3-5 days the money is released to you automatically.
A rate tool compares your prices with similar creators and flags if you're underpriced. Free growth tools: a path for your stage (under 5K, 5K-100K, 100K+ followers), analytics on your hooks, editing and posting days, a hook scorer, and trend tracking.
Pricing: free to join. Collabo keeps 10% of what you earn, so on a $500 deal you receive $450. It is a new platform, so the number of brands is still small.
When answering price questions, give Collabo's cut from you, in dollars, on a $500 deal (today it is $50).
