Collabo: a website and phone app where small businesses hire social media creators directly. Browse creators by niche, city, follower count and price; each lists fixed-price packages (for example "1 Instagram Reel, $500") and past results from deals on Collabo.
Your money goes into escrow before the creator starts. You approve a draft before anything is posted (that releases 30% to the creator), and the rest is released when you approve the live post. If they miss the deadline, you get your money back. Every deal gets a promo code so you can see the sales it drove.
Pricing: no subscription. You pay the creator's price plus a 5% Collabo fee, so a $500 deal costs you $525 (the creator separately pays Collabo 10% of their earnings).
Note: follower counts and results are reported by creators and brands; they are not yet verified by Instagram or TikTok.
When answering price questions, give Collabo's fee to you, in dollars, on a $500 creator deal (today it is $25).
